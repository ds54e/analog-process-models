## Scope of validation

This validates the nominal APM130 public kit and the common terminal
characterization methodology. Full numerical result directories remain
untracked and are regenerated by the command above. APM benchmark variation is
M4 work; IHP-native corners/process/mismatch are M8 work; Spectre remains
experimental/unverified M9 work. No all-kits or release-readiness gate is
claimed by this evidence alone.
