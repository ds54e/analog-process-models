## Numerical-method and behavior checks

Checks cover 1,469 above-criterion operating points from 0.25 to 0.9 VDD.

| Check | Median relative difference | 95th percentile | Criterion |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| gm, 10 mV vs 20 mV central difference | 0.0733% | 1.525% | p95 < 2% |
| gds, 10 mV vs 20 mV central difference | 0.0397% | 0.582% | p95 < 2% |
| terminal gm vs native BSIM4 gm oracle | 0.0358% | 0.629% | p95 < 2% |
| terminal gds vs native BSIM4 gds oracle | 0.0177% | 0.201% | p95 < 2% |

Native BSIM4 values are validation oracles only; terminal finite differences
are canonical and both perturbation results are persisted.

All conduction-region Id-Vg and Id-Vd groups are monotonic. Seven full-range
PMOS Id-Vg groups at -40 degC show transparent off-state leakage-partition
reversals only at VCTRL=0..40 mV. The largest one-step change is 12.35 pA,
orders of magnitude below the applicable 0.5..1 uA threshold criterion. These
signed terminal data remain in the raw curves and are reported in metadata;
they are not treated as channel-conduction failures.

All 24 DIBL extractions used `Id=100 nA * W/L`, VOUT low=50 mV, and VOUT
high=0.8 V. DIBL was positive throughout, spanning 0.00724..0.13529 V/V. At
27 degC it decreased with length for both polarities:

| Polarity | L/Lmin=1 | L/Lmin=2 | L/Lmin=4 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| N | 0.09175 | 0.01876 | 0.01169 |
| P | 0.11450 | 0.02490 | 0.01618 |

All 48 raw Y records preserve 16 complex entries at 100 kHz or 1 MHz. Both
frequencies exist for every geometry/temperature/polarity combination. Maximum
terminal KCL column-sum residual was `6.717e-12 S`; Cgg/Cgd/Cgs were positive,
and their maximum relative change between the two frequencies was
`2.11e-16` (roundoff-scale for this quasi-static run).

At 27 degC near gm/Id=15 1/V, gm/gds increased with length for both devices
(N: 11.49, 85.01, 221.63; P: 9.49, 67.74, 187.13). At W=1 um, minimum length,
and VCTRL=VOUT=1 V, ngspice produced 979.18 uA N and 653.13 uA P. Those values
are within 0.44% of the upstream manual's nominal 975.5 uA/um and 650.3 uA/um
VTG values, providing a direct interpretation check without asserting silicon
correlation.

## Scope of validation

This validates the nominal APM045 public kit and its native ngspice BSIM4
interpretation. Full numerical result directories remain untracked and are
regenerated by the command above. APM benchmark variation is M4 work; Spectre
remains experimental/unverified M9 work. No all-kits or release-readiness gate
is claimed by this evidence alone.
